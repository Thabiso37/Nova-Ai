🚀 Seller checklist

A good listing helps customers understand what you offer.

1. Use a clear product name.
2. Set an accurate price in rand.
3. Use your own product photos or authorised images.
4. Explain sizes, condition and delivery arrangements.
5. Respond to customer enquiries promptly.
