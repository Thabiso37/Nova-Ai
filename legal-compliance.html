2. About NOVAAI MARKET

NOVAAI MARKET is an online marketplace designed to connect independent sellers with potential buyers across three categories:

Retail

Products and merchandise advertised by independent sellers.

Motors

Vehicles and motor-related listings advertised by independent sellers.

Property

Property-related advertisements, including property offered for sale or rental.

NOVAAI MARKET provides digital infrastructure through which listings can be displayed and discovered. The seller remains responsible for the goods, vehicles or property advertised. NOVAAI MARKET does not become the owner of an advertised item merely because it appears on the platform.

3. How the marketplace operates

  1. Buyers may browse categories and listings.
  2. Sellers may apply for accounts and permission to advertise.
  3. Seller applications may be reviewed before approval.
  4. Approved sellers may create listings subject to marketplace rules.
  5. Buyers may use available contact, enquiry and platform features to communicate about listings.
  6. The platform may provide cart, checkout, order and fulfilment functionality. Legal and payment status must reflect the actual functionality enabled at the time.
  7. NOVAAI MARKET does not claim an order is paid, settled or completed unless that status is properly established.
  8. An order record alone does not prove that money has been received.
  9. Platform, buyer and seller responsibilities depend on the actual service and applicable South African law.

4. Business and revenue model

The intended model supports accessible marketplace participation.

4.1 Free marketplace access

  • Free seller registration and basic accounts.
  • Free basic Retail listings.
  • Free basic vehicle listings.
  • Free basic Property listings.

4.2 Optional seller advertising

NOVAAI MARKET intends to offer optional once-off paid advertising placements. Before purchasing, sellers will be told the price, placement, duration, activation conditions, payment method and applicable cancellation/refund conditions.

4.3 Fees not intended in the model

The intended model does not include seller subscriptions, memberships, recurring seller fees, basic listing fees or sales commissions.

4.4 Advertising revenue

Advertising fees are payment for NOVAAI MARKET's own advertising service, not commissions on goods, vehicles or property sold by independent sellers. Paid advertising will only be activated after applicable business, legal, disclosure and payment arrangements are addressed.

5. Buyer rules

Buyers should use the platform responsibly and:

  1. Review listing descriptions carefully.
  2. Confirm prices and availability with sellers.
  3. Ask about condition, ownership and relevant documents.
  4. Verify the seller's identity and authority.
  5. Inspect goods and vehicles where appropriate.
  6. Independently verify property information.
  7. Agree on payment, collection, delivery and transfer arrangements.
  8. Keep appropriate records of communications and transactions.

Buyers should not pay based solely on an advertisement without reasonable verification. Report suspicious advertisements, misleading descriptions or suspected fraud. Nothing here removes statutory consumer rights.

6. Seller rules

Sellers must provide accurate, lawful information and are responsible for listing accuracy; authenticity and condition; authority to advertise or sell; photographs and descriptions; pricing and availability; disclosure of material defects and restrictions; honest responses; legal compliance; and agreements made with buyers.

Sellers must not advertise stolen or counterfeit goods, advertise property without appropriate authority, impersonate others, publish fraudulent or misleading advertisements, misrepresent ownership or vehicle information, or use the platform unlawfully.

NOVAAI MARKET may review, restrict or remove listings that appear unlawful, fraudulent or inconsistent with platform rules. Seller approval does not automatically verify ownership, financial standing or ability to complete a transaction.

7. Retail marketplace notice

Retail listings are supplied by independent sellers. Unless expressly stated, NOVAAI MARKET does not manufacture, own, warehouse or independently inspect every advertised product. Sellers remain responsible for descriptions, condition, authenticity, pricing, applicable warranties, delivery arrangements and consumer obligations. Buyers should confirm specifications and condition before committing. This notice does not remove statutory consumer protections that may apply to the seller or platform.

8. Motors marketplace notice

Vehicle advertisements are provided by independent sellers or advertisers. Buyers should independently verify seller identity, ownership, registration documents, vehicle identification details, mileage, accident/damage history, roadworthiness, outstanding finance or legal restrictions, warranties and transferability. NOVAAI MARKET does not automatically certify that a vehicle is roadworthy, free of finance, correctly valued or legally transferable. Sellers are responsible for accurate vehicle information and applicable motor-industry requirements.

9. Property marketplace notice

Property advertisers must have authority to advertise and ensure listing accuracy. Buyers and prospective tenants should verify ownership, advertiser authority, property details, price, restrictions, title/ownership records and the identity and credentials of any property practitioner involved.

9.1 Property Practitioners Act

NOVAAI MARKET recognises the Property Practitioners Act 22 of 2019 and the regulatory role of the Property Practitioners Regulatory Authority (PPRA). The legal classification of the platform's Property activities must be assessed against its actual services and functions. Calling the platform an advertising venue does not itself establish exemption from registration or other requirements. NOVAAI MARKET will not claim practitioner registration or a Fidelity Fund Certificate unless obtained and verified. Where applicable, property practitioners advertising through the platform remain responsible for their statutory and professional obligations.

10. Marketplace intermediary and consumer protection notice

NOVAAI MARKET recognises the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 (CPA). The platform's legal classification depends on its actual role in transactions. Its activities must be assessed against the applicable intermediary definition and section 27 and Regulation 9 requirements. Where those requirements apply, prescribed disclosures will be made in the required manner. These may include intermediary identity and legal status, physical/postal addresses, contact information, applicable registration information, service description, fees/remuneration, other prescribed information and required transaction records. Where identity-number disclosure is legally required, a suitable method must be established that respects applicable law and personal-information protection. Publishing a general notice alone does not establish that all intermediary obligations have been met.

12. Advertising terms

NOVAAI MARKET may provide optional promotional advertising services to sellers. Advertising information will specify format, placement, price, duration, activation date, content rules, payment conditions and cancellation/refund arrangements. Advertisers must ensure content is accurate and lawful. NOVAAI MARKET may reject, suspend or remove advertisements that appear unlawful, misleading, fraudulent or inconsistent with platform rules. An advertising purchase does not guarantee a sale, enquiry, views or return. Fees will be disclosed before commitment and cancellation/refund conditions will comply with applicable law.

13. Payments and financial responsibilities

NOVAAI MARKET distinguishes payments for its own advertising from payments between buyers and independent sellers.

13.1 Advertising payments

Advertising payments relate to NOVAAI MARKET's promotional services and must use an appropriately authorised, approved arrangement when activated.

13.2 Buyer/seller payments

Third-party transaction payment arrangements must be clearly disclosed. NOVAAI MARKET does not claim to provide escrow, hold seller funds or guarantee settlement unless a separately approved, legally compliant service is introduced.

13.3 Status records

Creating an order does not mean payment is confirmed. Payment, refund, reversal and settlement records must accurately reflect verified events.

16. Privacy policy — Protection of Personal Information

NOVAAI MARKET aims to handle personal information lawfully, responsibly and securely in accordance with applicable data-protection requirements including POPIA.

16.1 Information that may be collected

  • Names and surnames, email addresses, telephone numbers and account information.
  • Seller application information and listing information.
  • Enquiry messages, order and fulfilment information.
  • General location information, device/browser information and security/access logs.

16.2 Purposes

Information may be processed to create/administer accounts, manage seller applications, publish listings, connect buyers and sellers, respond to enquiries, administer orders/fulfilment, provide advertising, keep accounting records, prevent fraud, protect security and comply with legal obligations.

16.3 Lawful processing

Personal information will be processed only where an applicable lawful basis exists, for specific and lawful purposes, and not retained longer than necessary unless a lawful reason requires it.

16.4 Sharing

Information may be shared as needed with relevant buyers/sellers, service providers supporting platform operations, approved payment providers where applicable, and regulators/public authorities where legally required. NOVAAI MARKET does not claim to sell users' personal information to data brokers.

16.5 Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures will be taken to protect information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. Access should be restricted to authorised people who need it for legitimate purposes.

16.6 Retention

Information will be retained as reasonably necessary for its purpose or as required by law. When no longer required, it should be securely deleted, destroyed or de-identified where appropriate.

16.7 Rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may request access, correction, deletion where permissible, information about processing, withdrawal of consent where consent is the lawful basis, and assistance with privacy complaints. Requests may be sent to novaaimarket@gmail.com.
