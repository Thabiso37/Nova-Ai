This Legal & Compliance Centre sets out the business identity, marketplace operations, advertising services, user responsibilities and privacy practices of NOVAAI MARKET.

NOVAAI MARKET operates in the Republic of South Africa as an online multi-category marketplace and digital advertising platform.

The platform provides an online environment where independent sellers can advertise products, vehicles and property and where potential buyers can discover listings and contact advertisers.

This document explains the responsibilities of NOVAAI MARKET, sellers, advertisers and buyers.

1. Business identity and legal information

Business name: NOVAAI MARKET

Business activity: Online multi-category marketplace and digital advertising platform.

Country of operation: Republic of South Africa.

Website: https://thabiso37.github.io/Nova-Ai/

General and business enquiries: novaaimarket@gmail.com

Customer and seller support: novaaimarket.support@gmail.com

NOVAAI MARKET is the business name under which the marketplace and its advertising services are presented to the public.

The business's legal operator and applicable registration details must be reflected accurately in its formal legal and business records. Nothing on this page represents NOVAAI MARKET as an incorporated company or claims a registration that has not been established.

NOVAAI MARKET recognises its obligations under applicable South African legislation, including consumer protection, electronic commerce, privacy and other laws relevant to the services it provides.

2. About NOVAAI MARKET

NOVAAI MARKET is a South African online marketplace connecting independent sellers and advertisers with potential buyers.

The marketplace provides three principal categories:

Retail: Products and merchandise advertised by independent sellers.

Motors: Vehicles and motor-related advertisements.

Property: Property advertisements, including property offered for sale or rental.

NOVAAI MARKET provides digital infrastructure for displaying, discovering and promoting advertisements.

The seller or advertiser is responsible for the goods, vehicles or property advertised and for ensuring that the information supplied is accurate and lawful.

The appearance of a listing on NOVAAI MARKET does not mean that NOVAAI MARKET owns the advertised item or property.

3. How the marketplace operates

NOVAAI MARKET provides a platform through which:

  1. Buyers browse products, vehicles and property.
  2. Sellers apply for accounts and relevant marketplace permissions.
  3. Seller applications may be reviewed before approval.
  4. Approved sellers create advertisements in their authorised marketplace category.
  5. Buyers discover listings and contact sellers.
  6. Buyers and sellers communicate directly.
  7. Buyers and sellers independently agree on the terms of any transaction.

Buyers and sellers are responsible for negotiating and arranging the sale, payment, collection, delivery, transfer and other transaction details between themselves.

NOVAAI MARKET's intended operating model is to provide marketplace access and advertising services. It does not intend to act as the seller of independently advertised goods, vehicles or property, receive buyer-to-seller transaction funds, operate escrow, manage seller payouts, collect sales commissions or arrange delivery.

The actual functionality made available on the platform must remain consistent with this operating model and applicable law.

The existence of technical features such as cart, checkout, order or fulfilment records does not, by itself, establish that a payment has been received or a transaction completed.

4. Business and revenue model

NOVAAI MARKET is designed to provide accessible marketplace participation.

4.1 Basic marketplace access

The intended basic marketplace model includes:

  • Free seller registration and basic accounts.
  • Free basic Retail listings.
  • Free basic Motors listings.
  • Free basic Property listings.

4.2 Optional paid advertising

NOVAAI MARKET intends to offer optional once-off paid advertising placements.

Sellers and advertisers may choose to purchase promotional visibility for their listings or businesses.

Before an advertising purchase is accepted, the advertiser must be informed of the applicable:

  • Advertising price.
  • Advertising format.
  • Placement.
  • Duration.
  • Activation conditions.
  • Payment arrangements.
  • Cancellation and refund terms.

Paid advertising must only be activated when the applicable legal, business and payment arrangements are in place.

4.3 Fees

The intended business model does not include:

  • Seller subscriptions.
  • Seller memberships.
  • Recurring seller fees.
  • Basic listing fees.
  • Sales commissions.

4.4 Advertising revenue

Advertising payments are intended to compensate NOVAAI MARKET for its own advertising and promotional services.

They are separate from payments made between independent buyers and sellers for goods, vehicles or property.

Purchasing an advertising placement does not guarantee a sale, enquiry, number of views or financial return.

The legal classification of NOVAAI MARKET depends on its actual activities and responsibilities, not simply on the description of its revenue model.

5. Buyer rules

Buyers using NOVAAI MARKET should take reasonable steps to verify advertisements and sellers before entering into transactions.

Buyers are encouraged to:

  1. Read listing descriptions carefully.
  2. Confirm prices and availability directly with sellers.
  3. Ask questions about condition, specifications and documentation.
  4. Verify the seller's identity and authority.
  5. Inspect goods and vehicles where appropriate.
  6. Independently verify property information and ownership.
  7. Agree directly with sellers on payment, collection, delivery and transfer.
  8. Keep records of relevant communications and agreements.

Buyers should not assume that an advertisement has been independently verified merely because it appears on the platform.

Suspicious advertisements, misleading information and suspected fraud should be reported to NOVAAI MARKET.

Nothing in these rules removes consumer rights provided by applicable South African law.

6. Seller rules

Sellers are responsible for the accuracy, legality and completeness of their advertisements.

Sellers must:

  • Provide truthful descriptions and photographs.
  • Advertise goods, vehicles and property they are legally entitled to advertise.
  • Provide accurate prices and availability.
  • Disclose material defects and relevant restrictions.
  • Avoid misleading statements about ownership, condition or specifications.
  • Respond honestly to buyer enquiries.
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations.
  • Honour agreements they enter into with buyers.

Sellers must not:

  • Advertise stolen or counterfeit goods.
  • Advertise property without appropriate authority.
  • Impersonate another person or business.
  • Submit fraudulent or misleading advertisements.
  • Misrepresent vehicle ownership or vehicle information.
  • Use the platform for unlawful activities.
  • Misuse another person's personal information.

NOVAAI MARKET may review, restrict, suspend or remove listings that appear unlawful, fraudulent, misleading or inconsistent with marketplace rules.

Approval of a seller account does not automatically verify ownership, professional qualifications, financial standing or the seller's ability to complete a transaction.

7. Retail marketplace notice

Retail advertisements are supplied by independent sellers.

Unless expressly stated otherwise, NOVAAI MARKET does not manufacture, own, warehouse or independently inspect every advertised product.

Sellers remain responsible for:

  • Product descriptions and specifications.
  • Product condition and authenticity.
  • Pricing and availability.
  • Applicable warranties and guarantees.
  • Delivery or collection arrangements.
  • Their consumer-protection obligations.

Buyers should confirm product details, condition, availability and applicable terms before committing to a purchase.

This notice does not exclude consumer protections that may apply under South African law.

8. Motors marketplace notice

Vehicle and motor-related advertisements are supplied by independent sellers or advertisers.

Buyers should independently verify:

  • Seller identity and authority.
  • Vehicle ownership and registration documents.
  • Vehicle identification details.
  • Mileage and service history.
  • Accident and damage history.
  • Roadworthiness.
  • Outstanding finance or legal restrictions.
  • Warranties and transferability.
  • Other information material to the proposed transaction.

NOVAAI MARKET does not automatically certify that an advertised vehicle is roadworthy, free from outstanding finance, correctly valued or legally transferable.

Sellers remain responsible for accurate vehicle information and compliance with applicable motor-industry requirements.

9. Property marketplace notice

Property advertisements must be submitted by persons who have the legal authority to advertise the relevant property.

Advertisers are responsible for ensuring that property descriptions, prices, photographs and other listing information are accurate.

Buyers and prospective tenants should independently verify:

  • Property ownership.
  • Advertiser authority.
  • Property details and condition.
  • Sale price or rental terms.
  • Ownership records and restrictions.
  • The identity and credentials of any property practitioner involved.

9.1 Property Practitioners Act

NOVAAI MARKET recognises the Property Practitioners Act 22 of 2019 and the regulatory role of the Property Practitioners Regulatory Authority (PPRA).

The legal classification of NOVAAI MARKET's Property activities depends on the actual services and functions performed.

Describing a platform as an advertising venue does not automatically establish an exemption from registration, licensing or other statutory requirements.

NOVAAI MARKET does not claim to be a registered property practitioner or to hold a Fidelity Fund Certificate unless such status has been obtained and verified.

Property practitioners advertising through the platform remain responsible for their own applicable statutory and professional obligations.

13. Payments and financial responsibilities

NOVAAI MARKET distinguishes between payments for its own advertising services and payments made directly between independent buyers and sellers.

13.1 Advertising payments

Advertising payments relate to NOVAAI MARKET's own promotional services.

Where paid advertising is activated, payments must be handled through an appropriately authorised arrangement, with applicable prices, terms and conditions disclosed to advertisers.

13.2 Buyer and seller payments

Under the intended operating model, buyers and sellers communicate directly and independently arrange payment for goods, vehicles or property.

NOVAAI MARKET does not intend to:

  • Receive buyer-to-seller transaction money.
  • Hold money on behalf of buyers or sellers.
  • Operate escrow.
  • Manage seller payouts.
  • Guarantee payment or settlement.
  • Collect sales commissions.
  • Manage delivery or collection payments.

Any future change to these arrangements requires a separate legal and operational assessment.

13.3 Order and payment records

An order record does not establish that payment has been received.

A listing, enquiry, order or fulfilment record must not be represented as proof that a transaction has been paid, settled or completed unless the relevant event has actually been verified.

Payment, refund, reversal and settlement information must accurately reflect verified events.
