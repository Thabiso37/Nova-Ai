Advertisement Management

Manage all nine NOVAAI advertising boards separately. Each board has its own campaigns, schedule and activation controls.

Create campaigns directly from your phone. Upload an image or MP4/WebM video to NOVAAI Storage, or reuse an existing public HTTPS media URL. Choose an internal NOVAAI destination below. Campaign management remains separate from orders, payments, seller balances and product records.
On Android, tap each board you want this campaign to appear on. You can select multiple boards. Choose a file from your Android phone. Maximum size: 100 MB. The secure NOVAAI Storage URL is generated automatically after upload.
No new media selected. For an existing campaign, its saved media is reused unless you upload a replacement.
NOVAAI generates the destination link automatically. No external website is required.
