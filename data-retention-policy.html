NOVAAI MARKET

Data Retention and Disposal Policy

How NOVAAI MARKET reviews, retains, restricts and disposes of personal information.

Published and effective: 27 November 2026 · Policy owner: Thabiso Putlane, proprietor and Information Officer of NOVAAI MARKET · Jurisdiction: Republic of South Africa.

Important: This policy governs NOVAAI MARKET’s own handling of information. It does not promise that an independent service provider immediately erases every copy from its systems or backups. Provider-specific retention and deletion arrangements must be checked against the applicable account settings and contracts.

1. Purpose and legal basis

NOVAAI MARKET is operated as a sole proprietorship by Thabiso Putlane. This policy applies to personal information processed for the NOVAAI MARKET website, Retail, Motors and Property marketplaces, seller administration, advertising services, support and security.

Under section 14 of the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), personal information must not be kept longer than necessary for its purpose, subject to lawful exceptions. When NOVAAI MARKET is no longer authorised to retain a record, it must be securely deleted, destroyed or de-identified as soon as reasonably practicable, in a way that prevents reconstruction in intelligible form.

2. Retention schedule

The following rules apply by information category. A legal hold, active complaint, security investigation, dispute, contract or statutory duty may justify retaining the minimum necessary records for longer. The reason and scope of any exception must be recorded and reviewed.

After closure, review the account record and remove or de-identify information no longer needed for account administration, security, a complaint or a legal obligation. Under section 29 of the Tax Administration Act 28 of 2011, the general five-year period depends on the taxpayer and record category, including five years from submission of a return for records under section 29(2)(a), or five years from the end of the relevant tax period for section 29(2)(c). 3. Review and disposal procedure

  1. Review information categories and outstanding records at least once every six months, and when an account closes, a listing ends, a verification decision is finalised, consent is withdrawn or a complaint is closed.
  2. Identify the purpose, lawful basis, record owner, applicable statutory period and any active dispute, investigation or legal hold before disposal.
  3. Where retention is no longer authorised, delete, destroy or de-identify the record as soon as reasonably practicable. Disposal must prevent the information from being reconstructed in intelligible form.
  4. Where deletion is temporarily restricted, limit access and further use to the purpose that justifies retention, record the reason and review the hold at least every six months.
  5. Keep a minimal disposal log recording the category, date, method, responsible administrator and any exception. Do not copy the personal information itself into the disposal log unless necessary.
  6. For information held by an operator or service provider, use the provider’s documented deletion process and retain evidence of the request or completion where available. Provider backup cycles and technical retention may differ and must not be misrepresented.

4. Access control and secure disposal

Only authorised persons with a legitimate operational need may access retained personal information. Records awaiting disposal must remain protected. Electronic records must be securely deleted or irreversibly de-identified where appropriate; physical records must be destroyed securely. Where a record is required only as evidence, access and processing must be restricted to that purpose.

5. Data subject requests

Individuals may request access to, correction of or deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable law and lawful retention exceptions. Requests may be sent to novaaimarket@gmail.com. NOVAAI MARKET will verify the requester as reasonably necessary, assess the request and respond under the applicable legal requirements.

6. Responsibility and review

The proprietor and Information Officer, Thabiso Putlane, is responsible for overseeing this policy, assigning authorised disposal tasks, recording exceptions and reviewing the policy when services, information flows, legal requirements or provider arrangements materially change.

This policy is intended to be applied together with the Legal & Compliance Centre, PAIA Manual and the privacy notices presented at relevant collection points. It does not override a statutory retention duty or create a right to delete records that the law requires NOVAAI MARKET to keep.
