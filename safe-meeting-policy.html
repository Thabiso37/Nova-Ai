NOVAAI MARKET POLICY

SAFE MEETING, VIEWING, COLLECTION AND EXCHANGE POLICY

1. Our Commitment to Safety

NOVAAI MARKET encourages every buyer, seller, tenant, landlord, vehicle buyer, vehicle seller and other marketplace participant to put personal safety first when arranging an in-person meeting, property viewing, vehicle inspection, product collection or exchange.

Buyers and sellers must use sensible precautions and take responsibility for their personal safety when arranging and attending meetings.

NOVAAI strongly recommends that all in-person meetings take place in safe, public, busy and appropriately populated locations.

A convenient meeting place is never more important than personal safety.

2. Recommended Safe Meeting Locations

Where practical, buyers and sellers should arrange to meet at places such as:

  • Shopping malls.
  • Shopping centres.
  • Busy cafés and restaurants.
  • Well-lit public areas.
  • Public locations with visible security personnel.
  • Designated safe exchange points.
  • Other busy, established public venues where assistance is readily available.

Choose locations that are open, populated, accessible and suitable for the type of transaction.

Avoid meeting at unfamiliar, isolated or deserted locations.

3. Meeting During Safe Hours

NOVAAI recommends arranging meetings during daylight hours or normal business operating hours wherever possible.

Buyers and sellers should avoid unnecessary late-night meetings, particularly in unfamiliar locations or places with limited public activity.

If a meeting must take place outside normal hours, participants should take additional precautions and choose a well-lit, populated and appropriate location.

4. Personal Safety Precautions

Before attending a meeting, buyers and sellers should:

  1. Tell a trusted friend, family member or another responsible person about the meeting.
  2. Share the meeting location and expected meeting time with that person.
  3. Arrange their own reliable transport where possible.
  4. Keep their mobile phone charged and accessible.
  5. Avoid carrying unnecessary cash or displaying valuable belongings.
  6. Never disclose banking passwords, PINs, one-time passwords or other confidential security information.
  7. Avoid sharing unnecessary personal information.
  8. Leave immediately if the location, circumstances or behaviour of another participant makes them uncomfortable.
  9. Never allow pressure, urgency or fear of losing a deal to override personal safety.

Where appropriate, buyers and sellers should consider taking a trusted person with them.

5. Retail Product Meetings and Collections

For Retail purchases, buyers and sellers should preferably arrange product inspections, collections and exchanges at safe public locations.

Recommended locations include shopping centres, busy public areas, designated exchange points and other suitable places with visible security.

Buyers should inspect products before completing an exchange where practical.

Sellers should not pressure buyers into visiting isolated locations or entering unfamiliar private premises.

Where collection from a private address is unavoidable, participants should take additional precautions, tell a trusted person and consider arranging for another person to accompany them.

6. Motors: Vehicle Viewings and Inspections

For vehicle transactions, buyers and sellers should arrange viewings and inspections at appropriate, safe and populated locations wherever practical.

Suitable locations may include:

  • Established vehicle dealerships or inspection facilities.
  • Secure public areas.
  • Well-lit locations with security.
  • Other appropriate locations where the vehicle can be inspected safely.

Buyers should not attend isolated locations alone to view or test a vehicle.

Before proceeding, buyers should independently check the vehicle, its documentation, ownership information and relevant transaction details.

Test drives should only take place when the parties have agreed on appropriate safety arrangements and the driver is legally permitted to drive the vehicle.

Never hand over money, documents or vehicle keys under pressure or in circumstances that appear suspicious.

7. Property: Property Viewings and Meetings

For property sales and rentals, buyers and prospective tenants should arrange viewings with the seller, landlord or an authorised representative.

Where possible, property viewings should take place during daylight hours.

Participants should:

  • Confirm the identity and role of the person arranging the viewing.
  • Tell a trusted person the property address and viewing time.
  • Consider taking someone with them.
  • Avoid attending unfamiliar or isolated properties alone.
  • Avoid entering a property if the circumstances appear unsafe.
  • Independently verify the property's details, ownership or authority to let or sell before making commitments or payments.

A property listing or viewing arranged through NOVAAI does not establish that the advertiser is the lawful owner or authorised representative.

8. Suspicious Behaviour and Unsafe Situations

Buyers and sellers should be alert to suspicious conduct, including:

  • Requests to meet in isolated or deserted places.
  • Pressure to meet secretly or at unusual hours.
  • Threats, intimidation or aggressive behaviour.
  • Unusual demands for advance payments.
  • Requests for confidential banking information.
  • Attempts to change an agreed meeting location at the last minute without a reasonable explanation.
  • Requests to enter unsafe premises or vehicles.
  • Any behaviour that creates reasonable concern for personal safety.

If a participant feels unsafe, they should cancel or leave the meeting.

If there is an immediate threat or emergency, move to a safer place and contact the appropriate emergency services or local authorities.

Users may also report suspicious activity to NOVAAI through the official support channel.

9. NOVAAI's Role and Limitations

NOVAAI MARKET provides an online marketplace that allows independent buyers and sellers to discover listings and communicate about potential transactions.

NOVAAI does not attend, supervise, arrange security for or physically monitor private meetings, property viewings, vehicle inspections, product collections or exchanges.

NOVAAI does not guarantee the safety of any meeting location or the conduct, identity, intentions or actions of marketplace participants.

The responsibility for choosing a suitable meeting location and taking appropriate personal safety precautions remains with the individuals involved.

Nothing in this policy removes or limits any rights or responsibilities that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.

10. Reporting Safety Concerns

Users who experience suspicious behaviour, threats, attempted scams or other safety concerns connected with a NOVAAI listing or interaction should report the matter through:

NOVAAI MARKET Support
Email: novaaimarket.support@gmail.com

When reporting a concern, users should provide relevant listing information and a description of the incident. They should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive personal information.

NOVAAI may review reports and take appropriate action within its marketplace role, including restricting or suspending access where justified under its applicable policies.

NOVAAI support does not replace emergency services or law enforcement.

11. Acceptance of This Policy

All buyers and sellers must be given access to this policy before accepting the applicable NOVAAI marketplace terms.

Users must actively acknowledge the applicable policies during registration or when acceptance is otherwise required.

Sellers must also acknowledge the Seller Policy and applicable marketplace-specific requirements.

By using NOVAAI MARKET, users are expected to act responsibly, respect other participants and follow the applicable safety requirements.

12. Seller Application Acknowledgement

Seller applicants are required to confirm that they have read and agree to follow this policy before submitting a seller application. The application records the policy version and acknowledgement time in the account registration metadata. Buyers are encouraged to read this policy before arranging a meeting, viewing, collection or exchange.

This acknowledgement does not transfer responsibility for a private meeting or transaction to NOVAAI MARKET and does not remove any legal duty that may apply to the platform or any participant.

NOVAAI MARKET SAFETY MESSAGE

YOUR SAFETY COMES FIRST.

Meet in shopping malls, shopping centres, busy cafés, well-lit public places with security or designated safe exchange points.

Avoid isolated places. Prefer daylight and normal operating hours. Tell someone you trust where you are going. Take someone with you where appropriate.

If something does not feel right, do not proceed. Leave and seek assistance.

A transaction is never worth putting your safety at risk.
