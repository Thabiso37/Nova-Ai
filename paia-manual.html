1. INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE

1.1 This manual is prepared for NOVAAI MARKET, a South African sole proprietorship operated by Thabiso Putlane, in accordance with section 51 of the Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000, as amended.

1.2 The manual explains the records held by NOVAAI MARKET, how members of the public may request access to those records, and how personal information is processed in connection with the marketplace.

1.3 The manual is intended to promote transparency and explain the procedures available to people who wish to exercise their rights under PAIA and POPIA.

1.4 Access to records is subject to applicable legislation. The existence of a record does not automatically mean that access must be granted. Certain records may be protected by privacy, confidentiality, commercial sensitivity, legal privilege or other lawful grounds for refusal.

1.5 In this manual, references to NOVAAI MARKET, the marketplace or the private body refer to the business operated by its proprietor, Thabiso Putlane.

4. RECORDS AVAILABLE WITHOUT A FORMAL PAIA REQUEST

4.1 Certain information is ordinarily available to the public through the NOVAAI MARKET website and its public communications.

These categories may include:

  • Public marketplace pages.
  • Publicly visible product listings.
  • Publicly visible property listings.
  • Public listing descriptions and photographs.
  • Publicly displayed seller contact details where provided for marketplace use.

7. PURPOSES FOR PROCESSING PERSONAL INFORMATION

NOVAAI MARKET may process personal information for the following purposes:

7.1 Creating and administering buyer and seller accounts.

7.2 Verifying seller accounts and helping reduce impersonation, fraudulent activity and misleading listings.

7.3 Publishing and managing marketplace listings.

7.4 Displaying seller-provided contact details and general location information.

7.5 Enabling buyers and sellers to communicate directly, including through WhatsApp.

7.6 Supporting marketplace features such as carts, orders and wishlists.

7.7 Operating advertising placements and administering advertising campaigns.

7.8 Measuring first-party advertising views and engagement where those features are enabled.

7.9 Operating NOVA AI marketplace search.

7.10 Responding to customer and seller support requests.

7.11 Responding to privacy enquiries and access-to-information requests.

7.12 Maintaining website security and investigating technical problems.

7.13 Preventing misuse of the platform.

7.14 Meeting legal, regulatory, accounting and record-keeping obligations.

7.15 Establishing, exercising or defending legal rights.

NOVAAI MARKET's marketplace model is based on direct buyer-to-seller communication. Buyers and sellers arrange their product-sale payments and delivery directly with one another.

Any advertising payments made to NOVAAI MARKET are separate from payments for products purchased from sellers.

9.3 The production Render Python backend is recorded in the service dashboard as hosted in Frankfurt, Germany.

9.4 The Supabase project is recorded as hosted in the EU West region, Ireland.

9.5 Groq AI Service and Data Retention

NOVAAI MARKET uses the Groq API to provide AI-powered features through NOVA AI.

NOVAAI MARKET has enabled Groq's Inference APIs Zero Data Retention (ZDR) setting. Groq's Global Zero Data Retention setting is disabled. The enabled Inference APIs setting applies to NOVA AI's inference requests. Other Groq services may have separate data-retention settings.

NOVAAI MARKET does not intentionally store users' AI conversations in its own database. However, this statement does not mean that no information is ever processed or temporarily handled by the AI service provider or hosting infrastructure.

Users should avoid entering passwords, identity-document numbers, banking details, or other highly confidential information into NOVA AI.

9.6 The exact processing locations, subprocessors and retention arrangements of external providers may vary according to their infrastructure, service terms and account settings.

9.7 NOVAAI MARKET does not sell personal information as a business model. Personal information is shared for the purposes described in this manual, where authorised or where otherwise permitted or required by law.

12. PROCEDURE FOR REQUESTING ACCESS TO RECORDS

12.1 A person who wishes to request access to a record held by NOVAAI MARKET must submit a request in the prescribed PAIA form applicable to a private body.

12.2 The relevant request form is the prescribed Form 2 — Request for Access to Record of Private Body, subject to any replacement or amendment issued under applicable regulations.

12.3 The request should identify:

  • The record being requested.
  • The form of access requested.
  • The requester's contact details.
  • Any additional information reasonably required to locate the record.

12.4 Requests should be submitted to the Information Officer:

Email: novaaimarket@gmail.com

Postal correspondence: NOVAAI MARKET STAND NO 74612, SONDERWATER, SEBOKENG, GAUTENG, 1983, SOUTH AFRICA.

12.5 A requester may be required to provide proof of identity where reasonably necessary.

12.6 A person requesting access on behalf of another person may be required to provide evidence of authority to act on that person's behalf.

12.7 The Information Officer will consider the request in accordance with PAIA and the applicable statutory time periods.

12.8 PAIA generally provides a period of 30 days for a decision, subject to lawful extensions and other applicable provisions.

12.9 If access is refused or partially refused, the requester will be informed of the decision and the applicable reasons, subject to the law.

12.10 Access may be refused where PAIA permits or requires refusal. This may include circumstances involving:

  • Another person's privacy.
  • Confidential commercial information.
  • Trade secrets.
  • Legally privileged records.
  • Safety considerations.
  • Other lawful grounds for refusal.

12.11 Where appropriate, NOVAAI MARKET will consider whether information can be separated from protected information so that partial access may be granted.

12.12 Any applicable request or access fees will be dealt with in accordance with PAIA and the prescribed fee regulations. The requester will be informed of applicable fees.

12.13 A requester who is dissatisfied with a decision may use the remedies available under PAIA, including lodging a complaint with the Information Regulator within the applicable time limits.

12.14 Current forms, guidance and complaint procedures are available through:

https://inforegulator.org.za/paia/

https://eservices.inforegulator.org.za/paiamanuals/default.aspx

13. RIGHTS UNDER POPIA AND PRIVACY REQUESTS

13.1 A data subject may exercise rights provided under POPIA, subject to applicable legal requirements.

These rights may include:

  • Requesting confirmation of whether NOVAAI MARKET holds personal information about them.
  • Requesting access to their personal information.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Requesting deletion of information where legally permitted.
  • Objecting to certain forms of processing where POPIA allows.
  • Exercising other rights provided under applicable data-protection legislation.

13.2 Privacy requests should be sent to:

Email: novaaimarket@gmail.com

13.3 The requester should provide enough information to identify the relevant account or record.

13.4 NOVAAI MARKET may take reasonable steps to verify the identity and authority of a requester before responding.

13.5 A data subject may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator if they believe their rights under POPIA have been infringed.

13.6 These rights do not override lawful retention obligations, the rights of other people or lawful grounds for refusing a request.
